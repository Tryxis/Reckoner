How the maths works Reference

  • Wound chart: 2+ if S is double T or more, 3+ if S beats T, 4+ if equal, 5+ if S is lower, 6+ if S is half T or less. Unmodified 1s always fail; unmodified 6s are critical hits and critical wounds, and always succeed.
  • Benefit of cover is −1 to the attacker's ballistic skill, so it does nothing against melee and never touches an invulnerable save. Stealth grants it in the open.
  • Ballistic-skill, hit-roll and wound-roll modifiers are each capped at ±1 and tracked separately, so cover plus a −1 to hit stacks to an effective −2.
  • The invulnerable save is used whenever it beats the AP-modified armour save. Devastating Wounds bypass both, leaving only Feel No Pain.
  • Damage resolves as: roll, add Melta, halve and round up, subtract reduction, minimum 1. Feel No Pain is rolled per point, and damage left over when a model dies is lost rather than spilling onto the next.
  • Models slain comes from 20,000 simulated attack sequences, so the last decimal drifts slightly between runs.
